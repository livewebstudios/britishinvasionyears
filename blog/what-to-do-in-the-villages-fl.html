What to Do in Town

What to Do in The Villages Before the Show

Show: Thursday, April 8, 2027

The Villages, FL. Thursday, April 8, 2027 at 7:00 PM.

While you're in town for the show, here's a few things worth doing in the area.

THE THREE TOWN SQUARES
Spanish Springs, Lake Sumter Landing, and Brownwood Paddock Square each run live music most nights of the week. Spanish Springs has the '50s and '60s look down to the storefronts, so it's the natural warm-up for this one. The Villages town squares

SILVER SPRINGS STATE PARK
About forty minutes north in Ocala, and the glass-bottom boats have been running there since the 1870s. You look straight down into the spring and it's clear the whole way to the bottom. Silver Springs

SHOLOM PARK
Also in Ocala, forty-four acres of gardens and quiet walking paths, and it's free. A good, slow hour before an evening show. Sholom Park
