What to Do in Town

What to Do in Holmdel Before the Show

Show: Saturday, December 19, 2026

Holmdel, NJ. Saturday, December 19, 2026 at 7:30 PM.

While you're in town for the show, here's a few things worth doing in the area.

HOLMDEL PARK & LONGSTREET FARM
Right in town, and the best few hours you'll spend outdoors in December. Longstreet Farm is a working 1890s farm with the animals still in the barns and the house kept the way it was, and the park's hill is the one every kid in Monmouth County heads for the second it snows. Bundle up and walk the trails. Monmouth County Parks

ALLAIRE STATE PARK & THE HISTORIC VILLAGE
About twenty-five minutes south sits a whole 1830s ironmaking town, still standing where they left it. Brick row houses, the blacksmith shop, the foundry, all of it worked by hand back when this corner of New Jersey ran on bog iron. Walk it in the cold and you'll have the place nearly to yourself. Allaire Village

SANDY HOOK
Half an hour east you run out of New Jersey and hit the ocean. The lighthouse out there has been burning since 1764, which makes it the oldest one still working in the country, and Fort Hancock sits right alongside it. Cold, empty and gorgeous this time of year. Sandy Hook, Gateway National Recreation Area
