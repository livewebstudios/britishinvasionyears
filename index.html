The Story

A Complete ’60s Musical Retrospective

The Show

The Greatest & Longest Running Show of Its Kind.

More than just the Beatles, this fab ’60s musical retrospective spans the entire first wave of the British Invasion era, chock full of the groovy sights and sounds from both sides of the Atlantic!

The British Invasion

First, you may hear chart toppers by iconic British groups such as:

The Zombies Dave Clark Five The Rolling Stones The Who The Moody Blues Herman's Hermits The Kinks & many others

As bassist and vocalist Bobby M. notes, "One of the reasons audiences keep coming back is because they get to see and hear our faithful recreations of songs by these beloved artists."

The American Response

The American musical response follows as the band takes you through an exciting high-energy set of hits by legendary artists like:

The Monkees The Beach Boys Steppenwolf Tommy James & the Shondells Frankie Valli & The Four Seasons Neil Diamond The Turtles and countless more

The Beatles Finale

This journey through the 1960s concludes with an extraordinary note-for-note finale paying homage to the Four Lads from Liverpool, The Beatles! Bobby M. explains, "We posed the question, 'What if the Beatles had to perform their music today only as the four-piece band they were?'" The result by this "Fab Foursome" is truly astounding.

The show has generated tremendous excitement across the nation and internationally. Band members are all seasoned singers and musicians, having performed alongside and shared the stage with:

Ringo Starr & his All-Star Band · Eric Clapton · Herman's Hermits · Tommy James & the Shondells · The Troggs · The Turtles · Ben E. King · Leon Russell · and more

The Band

Lifelong musician Bobby M. (bass guitar) credits seeing The Beatles' debut on Ed Sullivan for inspiring him to form a band and become a full-time musician. Over the years, a common career path would finally merge Bobby with the current lineup in the BIY!

Bobby developed the concept of an exciting, unique concert experience, meticulously recreating all the amazing hit songs he grew up listening to, complete with period accurate outfits and multimedia.

In that moment, the foundation for The British Invasion Years was forged… Seasoned keyboardist and guitarist Jon Wolf had caught wind of Bobby's idea and wanted in. He dropped "subtle hints" at recording sessions, like randomly playing pieces of The Beatles' Abbey Road medley. Well, Jon's plan worked and he was immediately brought on board.

When the band was searching for a drummer, Bobby's longtime friend David Hall happened to be available at just the right time, enthusiastically answering the call. David brings a great musical dynamic and musicianship that is needed to bring these songs to life during their live performances.

Rounding out the sound, Bobby and David had known guitarist Joe LaFragola, "Joey La," for many years and were very excited to finally have the chance to add his very powerful guitar playing and vocals to the group. They knew Joe would bring the house down and amaze their audience at every show.

With the lineup now complete, the "Four Boys Making All That Noise" launched its ’60s musical invasion!
